# ADR-005: Customer-first positioning

**Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** Accepted

## Context
The site was planned to win an IT Manager job. Since January 2026 I already hold a manager role, and I am allowed to take freelance work. I want the site to bring in freelance clients first, and still work for employers who look at it.

## Options
1. **Job-seeker only** — clear message, but ignores freelance work.
2. **Equal dual audience** — two messages compete on every page; neither is strong.
3. **Customer-first, employers second** — the site sells services; case studies and a CV link give employers what they need.

## Decision
Option 3.
- Accept work from any industry and any location.
- The message leads with **factories and growing businesses**, because that is where my proof is strongest. Other businesses are welcome; they are not excluded.
- Remote services are offered worldwide. On-site services only where I can travel — area: `[placeholder]`.
- Primary call to action: **Hire me for a project**. Secondary: **View CV**.

### Initial services (proposed — to confirm)
| Service | Proof |
|---|---|
| IT Health Check (entry offer) | I run infrastructure for two factories |
| Microsoft 365 setup | Company M365 accounts and licences |
| Business apps on Microsoft 365 | Visitor system on Forms + SharePoint + Power Automate; style tracking workbook |
| Network setup & fix (on-site) | Cabling to network design; Advanced Network Engineer |
| Server & backup (on-site + remote) | MCSA; backups with real restores |
| Software implementation support | Visitor system, payroll and ERP rollouts |

**Not offered yet:** on-call / monthly support (clashes with my full-time job), firewall configuration, n8n / AI automation (still learning — stays in Lab), website development, security audits.

## Consequences
- New **Services** page and a Services section on Home.
- Home hero rewritten for clients; the hexagon navigation section is to be removed.
- Case studies are the proof for both audiences — they stay result-first.
- Lab stays separate and is not sold as a service until a real project is delivered.
- Open question: Burmese text for Services and Contact (multi-language is out of scope for v1.0).
- New risks R9 and R10 in the risk register.
