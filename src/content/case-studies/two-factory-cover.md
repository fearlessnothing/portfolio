## Executive summary

In 2023 I supported IT only for the Pathein factory. Then the IT staff at the group's second factory, in Thanlyin (Yangon), all left. I went to Thanlyin to support its users on site and kept supporting Pathein remotely. Thanlyin's IT had not been looked after for a long time and there was no handover, so I had to fix many things from the first day while learning the whole system on my own. After about three months, I was appointed IT Executive for both factories.

## Context

- **Pathein factory:** nearly 60 users. I had looked after it every day, so it was in good shape.
- **Thanlyin factory:** more than 100 users, no IT staff left, and IT that had not been looked after for a long time.

## My role

- **On site at Thanlyin:** day-to-day IT support for more than 100 users, and fixing problems that had built up.
- **Learning without a handover:** worked out how the whole Thanlyin system was set up, on my own.
- **Remote for Pathein:** supported nearly 60 users with AnyDesk, TeamViewer, UltraViewer and phone calls.
- **Same-day trips:** when a problem could not be fixed remotely, I travelled to Pathein, fixed it, and came back to Thanlyin the same day.

## Challenges & risks

- **No handover:** nobody was left to explain how Thanlyin's IT was set up. Understanding the whole system alone was exhausting.
- **Built-up problems:** IT at Thanlyin had not been looked after for a long time, so there was a lot to fix from the start.
- **Two sites, one person:** more than 160 users in two places, with only one of them on site.

## Results

- Both factories kept their IT support for about three months.
- More than 100 users supported on site and nearly 60 remotely.
- Appointed IT Executive for both factories, the role I held until becoming IT Manager in 2026.

## Lessons learned

- **A well-kept site needs less of you.** Because Pathein had been looked after every day, I did not need to worry about it while I was away. Thanlyin, left alone for a long time, needed fixing from the first day.
- **Handover matters.** Without one, the next person has to learn everything from scratch.
