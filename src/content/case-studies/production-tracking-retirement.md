## Context

The Production Tracking System (PTS) is a group system. The Industrial Engineering (IE) and Cutting teams entered data into it every day, but production got little value back:

- only the summary was useful;
- the data was not accurate;
- the live dashboard was late, so people did not trust it.

Both factories asked to stop using it.

## My Role

I collected feedback from both factories, informed the group IT team in Sri Lanka, included the findings in an executive summary for the Director and proposed building a better replacement together. I then coordinated the system's retirement.

## Options Considered

1. **Keep using it as it is.** Teams would keep spending time on daily data entry with little return.
2. **Stop it and plan a better replacement together with the group IT team.** This was the option chosen in October 2026.
